Blocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Blocks does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data. The app requests no Android permissions and has no internet access at all.

What Blocks collects

Nothing. Because Blocks has no internet permission, it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere. It works fully offline.

What Blocks stores on your device

Blocks saves two things in its own private app storage, so your game is still there when you come back:

  • your best score;
  • the game currently in progress — the board, the shapes in your tray, and your score.

This data never leaves your device and is not readable by other apps. Uninstalling Blocks deletes it. If you have Android's backup enabled, this small save file may be included in your own Google account backup, under your control and Google's terms — nothing is sent to us.

Third parties

Blocks contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

Blocks is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
