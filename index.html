Blocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Blocks has no servers and sends nothing to us. It works fully offline. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you choose to send to Google Play Games if you open the leaderboards, and that goes to Google, not to us.

What Blocks stores on your device

Blocks keeps your game in its own private app storage, so it is still there when you come back:

  • your best scores and the history of your best score;
  • the games in progress (the board, the shapes in your tray, your score);
  • your daily challenge results, streak, statistics and badges;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, reminder time, block colours).

This data stays on your device and other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling Blocks deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, it may be included in your own Google account backup, under your control and Google's terms. Nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

Blocks asks for one permission, and only if you turn on the daily reminder: permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games, optional)

Leaderboards are off until you tap the trophy button. Nothing connects to Google Play Games before that. If you choose to use them:

  • Blocks signs in with your Google Play Games profile, and Google shares your gamer name and avatar with the game;
  • Blocks sends the scores of your finished games (classic boards, daily challenge, Weekend Blitz) to Google's leaderboards, where other players can see your gamer name and score according to your Play Games visibility settings;
  • Google's Play Games software may collect diagnostics and usage data to keep the service working. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not receive your email address or any other account details, and we do not store any of this data ourselves. You can delete your scores and your Play Games data for Blocks at any time from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Third parties

Blocks contains no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. The only third-party software is Google's Play Games library, used only for the optional leaderboards described above. We do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect any.

Children

Blocks is suitable for all ages. The game itself collects no data from anyone, including children. The optional leaderboards need a Google Play Games profile; for children with a supervised Google account, Google requires a parent's approval before a Play Games profile can be created.
